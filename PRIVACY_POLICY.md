# Privacy Policy — Brief AI

**Last updated:** March 3, 2026

## Overview

Brief AI is a Chrome extension that provides AI-powered text processing (summarization, translation, proofreading, chat, and live stream translation) using **Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano** model. All AI processing happens **locally on your device**.

## Data Collection

**Brief AI does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- No user accounts or sign-ins are required
- No analytics or tracking tools are used
- No data is sent to external servers
- No cookies are used

## Data Processing

All text processing (summarization, translation, proofreading, AI chat, and image description) is performed **entirely on your device** using Chrome's Built-in AI APIs (Gemini Nano). Your text never leaves your browser.

## Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `contextMenus` | Right-click menu for quick actions (summarize, translate, proofread) |
| `activeTab` | Access selected text on the current page when you use the extension |
| `scripting` | Inject content script for live caption translation on video sites |
| `storage` | Save your local preferences (language, mode) |
| `sidePanel` | Display the extension's main interface |
| `tabCapture` | Capture tab audio for live stream translation feature |

## Third-Party Services

Brief AI does **not** use any third-party services, APIs, or servers. All functionality relies on Chrome's native Built-in AI APIs.

## Data Stored Locally

The extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's `storage` API:

- Your preferred language settings
- Your selected AI mode (local)
- UI preferences

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the extension.

## Children's Privacy

Brief AI does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
